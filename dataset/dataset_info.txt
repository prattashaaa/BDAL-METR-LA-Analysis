METR-LA Traffic Dataset
=======================

Dataset Name:
METR-LA Traffic Dataset - 1 Million Records

Dataset File:
METR-LA_1million.csv

Number of Records:
1,000,000

Number of Attributes:
3

Attributes:
1. Sensor_ID
2. Timestamp
3. Traffic_Speed

Attribute Description:

Sensor_ID:
Unique identifier of the traffic sensor that recorded the measurement.

Timestamp:
Date and time at which the traffic measurement was recorded.

Traffic_Speed:
Recorded traffic speed at the corresponding sensor and timestamp.

Dataset Time Period:
March 1, 2012 to June 27, 2012

Measurement Interval:
5 minutes

30
